# Reading Log

## Chapter 5

### Biggest Idea
---
The biggest takeaway from this chapter was the concept of architectural _katas_ or essentially practice scenarios.  This allows for the building and understanding of explicit capabilities (things specifically needed based on the business domain and the requirements) and implicit ones (things that should be thought of over _any_ architectural solution).

It really emphasized with what should be taken away from architecture and decisions - there are no correct answers, but you must accept the tradeoffs or be prepared to accept the costs of all needed architectural decisions.

## Chapter 6

### Biggest Idea
---
One thing to truly understand with software architecture is the difficulty to properly measure it.  This chapter provided a number of tools that I have experience with at work as well as insight of the need for them.  Within a larger organization, and architect is almost like a captain of a ship for their particular org.  With a number of people underneath them, you can set up the right tools and frameworks to help govern the measurable metrics.

With independent work, the issue isn't the actual architecture itself, but more of self-discipline.  If/when I continue with my personal project - I will need to ensure that I've laid out my basis architectural decisions and mantras in order to keep myself well adjusted.
