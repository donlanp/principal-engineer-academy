# Principal Engineer Operating System
> A long-term roadmap for becoming an exceptional software engineer, architect, and technical leader.

---

# Vision

Become an engineer who is trusted to solve complex technical problems through thoughtful architecture, strong engineering practices, and continuous learning.

The goal is not mastery of a specific language or framework.

The goal is to become someone capable of designing, building, and leading complex software systems regardless of technology.

---

# Mission

Build deep expertise through deliberate practice.

Every topic studied should produce one or more tangible artifacts:

- Working software
- Design documents
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
- Technical writing
- Presentations
- Benchmarks
- Experiments

Learning is only complete when something has been built and documented.

---

# Core Principles

## Build > Consume

Reading creates awareness.

Building creates understanding.

For every hour spent learning, spend at least two hours building.

---

## Learn in Public

Whenever possible, document decisions.

The goal is not to become an influencer.

The goal is to create a searchable history of lessons learned.

---

## Think in Systems

Focus on understanding:

- Tradeoffs
- Constraints
- Scalability
- Maintainability
- Reliability
- Simplicity

rather than memorizing frameworks.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

This repository is intended to grow over many years.

Technologies will change.

Engineering principles will not.

---
